# upsc-trend-brief

A ten-minute research pass that ends in a short, ranked, dated brief. Never pad it; if a day is quiet, say so.

## Run

1. **Exam calendar first.** Search for anything that moved in the last 7 days or is due in the next 14:
   UPSC (CSE prelims/mains/interview, IFoS, CDS, NDA, ESE, CAPF), NTA (UGC NET, CUET, NEET), SSC CGL/CHSL,
   RBI/SBI/IBPS, the user's state PSC if known. Results, notifications, admit cards, deadline closes,
   answer keys, date changes, court cases about an exam. One search per board; extended mode.
2. **Current affairs with GS mapping.** Pull today's and yesterday's Indian Express UPSC Key, PIB
   headlines, The Hindu / Indian Express editorials, PRS bill tracker, Supreme Court and Parliament
   items. Keep only what can be taught in 45 seconds and tag each with GS1/GS2/GS3/GS4 or Paper 1.
3. **Creator radar.** Search for viral or controversial items in the exam space this week: topper
   interviews, coaching controversies, result-day reels, "aspirant life" trends, format trends
   (POV text reels, chronology reels, notebook reels). Note the format, not just the topic.
4. **Rank.** Score each idea 1 to 5 on three things: time-sensitivity (does it die in 48 h?), search
   demand (will aspirants look for it for months?), and fit with the creator's series (history
   chronologies, Paper 1 teaching, UPSC prep advice). Lead with the items that are both urgent and
   on-brand.
5. **Write the brief** in the creator's language (default Hinglish headings, English body):
   - Date line and one sentence on the day's mood.
   - "Post this week" : 2 to 3 urgent items, each with a hook line, GS tag, why now, and the skill to
     use to make it (history-explainer, data-story, reel-editor, favstash-write-script).
   - "Current affairs with reel potential" : 2 to 4 items, same fields.
   - "Evergreen" : 1 item that fits the running series.
   - "Could not verify" : anything that rests only on coaching-site blogs; name the official site to
     check (upsc.gov.in, ugcnet.nta.nic.in, pib.gov.in).
   - Sources as links.
6. If run on a schedule, end with the three strongest hook lines in one short paragraph so a push
   notification carries them.

## Rules

- Dates and numbers come from official sites or two independent outlets; otherwise mark unverified.
- Never invent a "viral reel". If nothing surfaced, say nothing surfaced.
- Prefer ideas that reuse the creator's existing material (their Paper 1 book, their history series)
  over ideas that need new research from zero.
- Keep the whole brief under 400 words.
